Class Timetable

Day Time Class
Tuesday 7:00 – 8:00 PM Women's Only Muay Thai

Our women's only class runs at Nolan Muay Thai in Beckenham every Tuesday from 7pm. It is a women-only session from start to finish, coached in a supportive environment where everyone works at their own pace.


All levels are welcome, including complete beginners. You do not need any experience, any fitness background, or any equipment to start — just trainers and water. You will learn genuine Muay Thai technique: stance, footwork, punches, elbows, knees and kicks, along with pad work and conditioning.


£15 drop-in — no membership or contract needed to try a class.

Why Train in a Women's Only Class?

Improve Fitness

Muay Thai is full-body conditioning that does not feel like a gym session. Pad work, bag work and drills build strength, stamina and coordination from your first class.

Build Confidence

Learning to strike properly changes how you carry yourself. You pick up practical self-defence skills alongside real technique, and progress is easy to feel week to week.

Reduce Stress

An hour on the pads is one of the most effective ways to clear your head. Plenty of our members come for the training and stay for how it makes the rest of the week feel.

Supportive Environment

A women-only room makes a difference if you are new to combat sports, or would simply rather train without a mixed class. Nobody is watching, and everyone starts somewhere.

Common Questions

Do I need any experience?
No. The class is built for all levels, and most people arrive having never trained before.


What should I bring?
Trainers, comfortable clothing and water. Gloves and pads are available if you do not have your own yet.


Is the class women only?
Yes. The Tuesday 7pm session is women only from start to finish.


Do I have to join as a member?
No. It is £15 to drop in to a single class. Memberships are available if you decide to train regularly.


Where is it?
Nolan Muay Thai, 9-10 Goodwood Parade, Upper Elmers End Rd, Elmers End, Beckenham BR3 3QZ — a few minutes from Elmers End station, and easily reached from Bromley, Penge, Crystal Palace, Sydenham and across South East London.
